Recordings were shortened to at most 40 seconds, crossfaded across the loop seam, resampled to 32 kHz stereo and encoded as Vorbis. No code from Noisekun is included. Retrieved 2026-11-16.

`white.ogg`, `pink.ogg`, `brown.ogg` are deterministic synthetic noise generated for this project with FFmpeg's `anoisesrc` (seed 42, amplitude 0.18, 32 kHz, 20 seconds), with no third-party samples. These loops are released under CC0.

## Fireplace layer

- `fireplace.ogg`: **Fireplace Sound loop**, by **PagDev**.
- Primary source and CC0 declaration: https://opengameart.org/content/fireplace-sound-loop
- Original WAV: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/fire.wav
- License: CC0 1.0. Retrieved 2026-11-16.
- Derived from the first 28 seconds, with a two-second crossfade across the loop seam, then resampled to 32 kHz stereo and encoded as Vorbis (26-second loop).
- Selected as a freely redistributable wood-burning fireplace ambience similar in subject to the user's Novaden YouTube reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4YCFhFKRZsc. Audio from that reference video is not bundled.
